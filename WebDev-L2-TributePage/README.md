### 📌 Project Overview
A dignified, responsive editorial tribute page honoring Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (1931–2015), the 11th President of India and renowned aerospace visionary. The site chronicles his humble beginnings, pioneering contributions to Indian space and defense systems, tenure as the People's President, and lifelong dedication to teaching.

---

### ✨ Feature Checklist (As per Oasis Infobyte Specification)
- [x] **Page Title & Inspiring Tagline**: Clear subject naming, birth/passing dates, and celebrated quote tagline.
- [x] **Prominent Portrait Frame**: Stylized high-fidelity portrait card complete with golden aura accents and credentials.
- [x] **Comprehensive Biography**: 4 original, in-depth paragraphs covering Rameswaram origins, DRDO/ISRO milestones (SLV-III, Agni, Prithvi), the People's Presidency, and his Vision 2020 educational legacy.
- [x] **Chronological Timeline**: Vertical milestone tree with 9 critical career dates from 1931 to 2015.
- [x] **Distinct Quote Showcase**: Styled interactive quote carousel with next/prev buttons, navigation indicator dots, and auto-rotation.
- [x] **Multiple Background Colors**: 5 distinct, harmonious background colors applied across sections (`--bg-hero`, `--bg-bio`, `--bg-timeline`, `--bg-quotes`, `--bg-honors`).
- [x] **Dual Typography Styles**: Elegant editorial Serif font (`Playfair Display`) for headings paired with clean Sans-Serif font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) for body readability.
- [x] **National & International Honors**: Highlights major civilian decorations including the Bharat Ratna (1997), Padma Vibhushan (1990), Padma Bhushan (1981), and the Von Braun Award (2013).
- [x] **Fully Responsive**: Adapts gracefully across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
