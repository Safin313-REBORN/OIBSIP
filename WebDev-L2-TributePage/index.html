INSPIRING LIFE CHRONICLE

The Journey of a Visionary

01

Humble Beginnings in Rameswaram

Born into a humble Tamil Muslim family in the island town of Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, A.P.J. Abdul Kalam learned the virtues of perseverance and humility at an early age. His father, Jainulabdeen, was a boat owner, and his mother, Ashiamma, a homemaker. To support his family's livelihood and pay for his schooling, young Kalam distributed newspapers in the early dawn before heading to class, demonstrating an unyielding thirst for knowledge that would define his entire existence.

02

The Missile Man at ISRO & DRDO

After graduating in aerospace engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT), Dr. Kalam joined the Aeronautical Development Establishment of DRDO. In 1969, he transferred to ISRO, where he served as the Project Director of India's inaugural Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III), successfully deploying the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit in 1980. His leadership in the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) gave India the Prithvi and Agni strategic missile systems, establishing the nation's self-reliance in sovereign defense.

03

The People's President

In 2002, Dr. Kalam was elected as the 11th President of India with overwhelming cross-party support. He transformed Rashtrapati Bhavan from an ivory tower of protocol into an accessible sanctuary for ordinary citizens, especially students, farmers, and innovators. He personally read and answered thousands of letters from children, advocated fiercely for technology-driven rural empowerment (PURA), and consistently championed universal education and self-reliance.

04

Eternal Teacher & Vision 2020

Following his presidency, Dr. Kalam embraced his true calling as an educator, serving as a visiting professor across India's premier institutes including IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Shillong, and Anna University. Through his landmark roadmap "India 2020," he articulated a concrete blueprint for transforming India into a developed nation by harnessing renewable energy, science, and the boundless potential of the youth. On 27 July 2015, while lecturing students at IIM Shillong, he collapsed and passed away doing what he loved most: teaching.

CHRONOLOGY OF EXCELLENCE

Key Achievements & Timeline

1931

Birth in Rameswaram

Born on October 15 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, to a humble family of seafaring boatmen.

1960

Aeronautical Engineering Graduate

Graduates from the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT) and joins DRDO as a scientific researcher.

1969

ISRO Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III)

Appointed Project Director of India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle at ISRO.

1980

Rohini Satellite Deployment

Successfully injects the Rohini satellite into orbit, cementing India's membership in the global space club.

1982

Director of DRDL & Missile Programs

Spearheads the development of India's strategic defense systems: Agni, Prithvi, Akash, and Nag.

1997

Conferred the Bharat Ratna

Honored with the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor, for scientific and technological leadership.

1998

Pokhran-II & Kalam-Raju Coronary Stent

Leads scientific execution of Pokhran-II nuclear tests and co-develops low-cost coronary stents with Dr. Soma Raju.

2002

11th President of the Republic of India

Sworn in as President of India, winning nationwide adoration as the "People's President."

2015

A Life Given to Education

Delivers his final words to young students at IIM Shillong before resting in eternal peace.

RECOGNITION & LAURELS

National & International Honors

🎖️ 1981

Padma Bhushan

Conferred by the Government of India for distinguished service in space research and launch vehicle systems.

🎖️ 1990

Padma Vibhushan

Awarded for extraordinary contributions to India's defense technologies and guided missile development.

🏅 1997

Bharat Ratna

India's highest civilian decoration, recognizing his pivotal leadership in modernizing sovereign defense and space sciences.

🌐 2013

Von Braun Award

Awarded by the National Space Society (USA) for outstanding leadership in space-related exploration and management.

An Immortal Light for the Youth of the World

The United Nations declared Dr. Kalam's birthday, October 15, as World Students' Day to celebrate his dedication to education and students worldwide.
